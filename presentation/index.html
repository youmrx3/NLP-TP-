Does an LLM-generated corpus
help classify Darija?

Text generation (NLG) + classification (NLU) of sentiment and disinformation

Group 2 — Kadouria Youcef · Atia Nour · Islam Cherif El Khir

ContextWhy Darija is a real challenge

Little data

Low-resource language: very few labelled corpora, no standard benchmark.

No fixed spelling

Arabic script and arabizi: 3, 7, 9, 5 = Arabic letters. "mli7a" = "مليحة".

Code-switching

French + Arabic in the same sentence:
service top ماشاء الله

➜ Idea: make data with an LLM, then measure whether it is really useful.

Assignment & research questionTwo steps, one question

NLG — Generation

An LLM generates a small Darija corpus (with code-switching):

  • varied sentiments
  • or fake news / real news

NLU — Understanding

A classifier predicts:

  • the polarity (positive / negative / neutral)
  • or the veracity (fake / real)
Question: does training a classifier on the generated corpus give good results on reference sentences (the "gold")?

Step 1 · NLGThe prompt that generates Darija

Example sentence (positive sentiment, code-switching):

  • Role: "native Darija speaker"
  • Imposed mix: 40% Arabic · 30% arabizi · 30% FR/AR
  • Variety: studies, sport, health, transport…
  • Few-shot: 4 gold examples in the prompt
  • JSON output → automatic parsing
  • Safety: harmless content, no real people

Error analysisWhere does it break?

  • Negation: "mchi mliha" understood as "mliha".
  • Positive ↔ neutral / negative: short sentences without a clear keyword.
  • Arabizi: spelling variants rare in the corpus.

Real examples

wallah l'connexion mchi mliha، kol nhar tweqef

actual: negative ➜ predicted: positive

la livraison كانت سريعة و le livreur جد محترم

actual: positive ➜ predicted: negative

Limits & methodological honestyWhat our results do not say

  • Small samples: gold of 48 / 20 sentences → margin of error of ±10 points or more.
  • Gold and LLM corpus written with the help of an LLM: easier test than real social-media Darija.
  • Synthetic label = intention of the prompt, not human annotation.
  • Disinformation: we learn the style, not the truth.

ConclusionWhat to remember

1

An LLM-generated corpus helps: 35% → 85% in sentiment.

2

A repetitive corpus (templates) teaches less: 69%. Generation quality matters.

3

Next: human annotation, real collected texts, XLM-R / DziriBERT, mental health in dialect.
